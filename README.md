## 🇬🇧 About Me
Zootechnician and **PhD candidate** at UNESP‑FMVZ (Botucatu, Brazil). I work on **ruminant nutrition**, **slow‑release urea technology**, and **meat quality**, combining **experimental design** and **data science** for sustainable production.

- **PhD project:** Slow‑release urea encapsulated in vegetal wax for confined lambs.  
- **Keywords:** rumen fermentation; carcass & meat quality; PCA; meta‑analysis; evidence synthesis.

### Featured Projects
- 🐑 **Lamb ULL Feedlot Analysis** — performance, digestibility & economics → <a href="https://github.com/francisconeto196/lamb-ull-feedlot-analysis">Repo</a>  
- 🥩 **Meat Quality PCA** — multivariate sensory analysis → <a href="https://github.com/francisconeto196/meat-quality-pca">Repo</a>  
- 📊 **Slow‑Release Urea Meta‑Analysis** — systematic review/meta‑analysis → <a href="https://github.com/francisconeto196/slow-release-urea-meta-analysis">Repo</a>
